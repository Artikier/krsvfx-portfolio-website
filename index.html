About

Hello!

I’m Kieran, a UK-based VFX artist currently working at CHIEF Productions as a Visual Effects Generalist.

Across the site you can find my professional work, personal projects and an archive of work from my time studying at Manchester Metropolitan University.

Skills across the VFX pipeline

  1. 3D

  2. Maya

    • Create polygonal assets, then rig and animate them.

    Houdini

    • Build procedural geometry and Houdini Digital Assets (HDAs).

    Nomad Sculpt

    • Sculpt digital forms.

  3. Substance Painter

    • Paint PBR textures and author materials and texture maps.

  4. Arnold, Karma & V-Ray

    • Develop shaders and material looks.
    • Light and render CG and FX.
  5. FX

  6. Houdini

    • Create RBD, FLIP and Pyro simulations, particle systems and grooming effects.

    Unreal Engine 5 & Niagara

    • Create environments and scene layouts, set up lighting and shaders, and build real-time visual effects with Niagara.
  7. Compositing

  8. 3D Equalizer

    • Solve 3D cameras and establish matchmove.

    Nuke

    • Track 2D motion for compositing.

    KeenTools FaceTracker

    • Track facial movement and support CG face integration.

  9. Silhouette

    • Create spline roto and refine mattes.

    Nuke

    • Clean up roto and generate mattes with CopyCat.

  10. Nuke

    • Pull keys, remove rigs, clean up plates and create digital makeup.

    ComfyUI

    • Generate AI-assisted paint elements.

    Photoshop

    • Prepare image patches.

  11. Nuke

    • Integrate CG into plates, match colour, balance shots and complete the final composite.
